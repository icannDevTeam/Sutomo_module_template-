Explore approval & roster →

Three distinctions that matter.

Interest is not assignment: clicking Accept records interest. The principal makes the final choice.

Assignment is not approval: picking an interested teacher does not approve the leave. Approval is a separate action unless the substitute was pre-picked during submission.

Coverage is a projection: the substitution roster is read-only. A missing matching duty produces a hand-off gap, even when the leave already has a substitute.
